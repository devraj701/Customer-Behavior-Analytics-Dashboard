Customer-Behavior-Analytics-Dashboard

This Customer Behavior Analytics Dashboard is an end-to-end Data Analytics project built using MySQL, Python, and Power BI. It helps businesses understand customer purchasing behavior, subscription status, revenue trends, and product performance through interactive dashboards and business insights. The dashboard enables organizations to make data-driven decisions that improve customer experience and business growth.

Description

The Customer Behavior Analytics Dashboard provides a complete analysis of customer purchasing patterns by combining MySQL for data querying, Python (Pandas & NumPy) for data cleaning and transformation, and Power BI for interactive visualization.

The dashboard includes important business metrics such as total customers, total revenue, purchase amount, subscription status, product category performance, and customer behavior trends.

Key Features
Customer Behavior Analysis

Analyzes customer purchasing patterns to understand customer preferences and buying behavior.

Revenue Analysis

Tracks total revenue and purchase amount to measure business performance.

Subscription Analysis

Shows subscription status and customer engagement trends.

Product Category Performance

Identifies top-performing product categories based on customer purchases.

Interactive Dashboard

Provides KPI cards, charts, and slicers that allow users to filter data by different categories for better analysis.

Benefits of This Dashboard
